# letsgo.casa pitch

We've all been out with a friend, when it feels like it's time to go home. But they seem like they're having such a good time, so you don't say anything. But then it turns out, they're thinking the same thing! Nobody wants to be the one who says it first.

With Let's Go Home, nobody has to.

You and your friend can each tell our web app when you're ready to go, without letting the other know until you both agree independently.

Go to letsgo.casa, and create a party. Give your friend the code, and they can enter it on their phone.

When you're ready, just check the box. You can change your mind if you want. When your friend checks the box on their phone too, Let's Go Home will let you both know that it's time to go. If your friend wants to stay, you don't have to ever let them know, so they can keep having a good time. It works regardless of who wants to go first. 

Behind the scenes, neither phone knows the other phone's status until they say they're ready to go, so there's no cheating. Each phone connects to our Node.js server, and never to each other directly. There are no accounts, and there's nothing to download. Just visit letsgo.casa on your phone and get started.
